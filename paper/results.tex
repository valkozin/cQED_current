\paragraph*{Cat state and macroscopic tunnelling.}
Without a seed $H$ is real, $\langle I\rangle=0$ in every eigenstate, and the order is carried by the two
lowest states of the doublet sector [Fig.~\ref{fig:udot}, $U=2\Delta$, $E_J/\hbar\omega=10$]: their
splitting $\delta$ collapses and the flux projected on them acquires the eigenvalues $\pm\Phi^\star$. DMRG
and the circuit model give the same $\Phi^\star$ to $0.2\%$ ($0.59\,\Phi_0/2$ at $\beta=2$), the same photon
number to $0.03$, and $\delta/\hbar\omega=0.70$, $0.24$, $2.65\times10^{-4}$ at $\beta=0.5$, $1$, $1.5$; at
$\beta=1.5$ the DMRG values at $\chi=300$ and $400$ are $2.66$ and $2.65\times10^{-4}$, so the macroscopic
tunnelling of a condensate of seven photons ($\delta=2\times10^{-7}\Delta$) is resolved directly. Deeper in
the ordered phase $\delta$ falls below the DMRG resolution for excited states ($4\times10^{-5}\hbar\omega$) and
the circuit model, diagonalized in the two flux-parity sectors separately, gives $8\times10^{-7}$ and
$6.5\times10^{-9}$ at $\beta=2$ and $3$ (SM). The DMRG ground state is the cat state itself:
$|\langle\Phi\rangle|<10^{-3}\Phi^\star$, $P(\Phi)$ is bimodal with peaks at $\pm\Phi^\star$
[Fig.~\ref{fig:udot}(c)], and a DMRG started from the polarized coherent state relaxes to the same symmetric
state to $10^{-10}\Delta$. With increasing $E_J/\hbar\omega$ the crossover sharpens into a pitchfork at
$\beta=1$ and $\delta$ vanishes exponentially [Fig.~\ref{fig:udot}(b)].

The two current directions are related by time reversal and $\delta$ is the only scale that separates
them. In the spin-degenerate model each is also Kramers degenerate in the spin of the dot, giving the
manifold $\{\uparrow,\downarrow\}\otimes\{\circlearrowright,\circlearrowleft\}$; with spin--orbit coupling,
as in InAs or Ge, the doublet surfaces are spin dependent~\cite{Padurariu2010,Tosi2019,Hays2021}, the wells
are spin locked and the manifold is one Kramers pair: the loop is then the inductively protected spin qubit
of Ref.~\cite{delOlmo2026}, of which Eq.~\eqref{eq:H} with a spin-dependent $t_J$ is the microscopic model.
A flux offset $\delta\Phi>\delta/2I^\star$ polarizes the loop in any realization; what the intrinsic $\pi$
junction removes is the need for a $\Phi_0/2$ bias, not the sensitivity to the bias point. A weak link
biased by $\Phi_0/2$ gives the same ordered state (SM), but its direction is selected by the residual $h/e$
harmonic, which tilts the double well by $0.2\hbar\omega$ in a ring of $14$ sites.
